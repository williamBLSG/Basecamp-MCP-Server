# Nashville CARES — Year in Review
### Marketing & brand partnership · prepared by The Roster

> **Updated 7/14/26** with the 2025 Social Report.

*The question this review answers: **what did this year of work buy Nashville CARES?** Not how many things we made — what changed for the organization, its mission, and the people it serves.*

---

## The headline: a year that built an audience, not just activity

This was the year Nashville CARES stopped being "the HIV organization" in the market and started being a **visible, modern, comprehensive-health brand that Middle Tennessee sees everywhere** — and, critically, an organization that now **owns audiences it can re-engage all year**, at nonprofit-beating efficiency.

Four things CARES got this year:

1. **A live second front door.** cares360.org launched — a full clinic website with Athena patient-portal login and online appointment booking, now serving **both Nashville and Memphis**. Patients can find and book care without picking up the phone.
2. **Real money, raised efficiently.** Dining Out For Life delivered **$86,592.98** in restaurant donations to CARES (final figure higher) — in a year Winter Storm Fern shut down the restaurant community weeks before recruitment.
3. **The best-performing social program in CARES' history** — organic engagement up **+323%** (May–Oct 2025), now benchmarked in the **top 15% of nonprofit social programs nationally**, with a paid layer reaching **112,000+ Nashvillians for $0.43 a click**.
4. **Audiences that keep working.** Pride's geo-fenced audiences are **retargeting for the clinic through December** — the June spend is still driving people to CARES 360 services in the fall.

## What we did, and what it delivered — initiative by initiative

*(Each section leads with the outcome for CARES.)*

### 1. Events — presence turned into dollars, patients, and community

**What CARES got:** the year's clearest, most measurable value — money raised, patients routed to services, and a citywide presence people could see and touch.

- **Dining Out For Life 2026** (Apr 30) — **$86,592.98 raised** across **65 restaurants** and **51 volunteer hosts**; **5.5M+ billboard impressions**, **372K paid + 438K organic** social impressions, **~33K email opens**, and **2 earned-media TV segments** plus a *Nashville Scene* full page. Impressions **beat the annual goal even as Winter Storm Fern** forced the restaurant count below the 2× stretch target — DOFL became the hospitality sector's "bounce-back" moment.
- **Pride 2026** (3 June Prides: RuCo, Upper Cumberland, Nashville flagship) — a full branded activation (big-top tent, misting arch, 36 poster-art chairs), **~307K impressions**, **860 clicks straight to CARES services**, and a paid layer that cost only **$317** — and is *still* retargeting clinic audiences through December. Co-branded on-site with **NPS Pharmacy** to route patients to care.
- **CARES 360 Donor Open House / Brand Launch** (Jan) — the public unveiling of the new brand and the 633 Thompson Lane clinic; executed end-to-end **(129/129 tasks complete)** with a recap video.
- **Memphis market entry (MC360)** — marketing support for CARES 360 taking over "The Corner" clinic via the Friends For All partnership, including the **Memphis Pride "Big Gay Dance Party"** and a May 11 clinic open house — extending the CARES footprint to a second city.
- **Also delivered through the year:** HIV Advocacy Day on the Hill, Nashville SC Pride Night / Hero of the Match, and the *Amna – TN Pride Business Leader of the Year* campaign.
- **In production:** the **40th Anniversary "Dreamscape" Gala (Nov 14)** — strategy, benchmarking, and creative foundation done, sponsor/invitation assets in production; and **AIDS Walk**, now kicking off.

### 2. Social media — the brightest measurable win

**What CARES got:** a genuine engagement engine, built from scratch, that turns a modest budget into outsized reach and an audience CARES can keep talking to.

**Where we started (June 2025 — Pride).** The first proof this engine could work came a year earlier, during Pride 2025: Instagram engagement hit **23%** — a then-record, up from **12.3%** at the start of that year — with **~3,500 monthly engagements** (more than double the **~1,600** Q1 baseline) and a single record day of **2,247 interactions**, all on a lean **$1,458** paid layer (Meta led at a **2.71% CTR** against a ~1.5% norm). Everything below is what that foundation grew into over the following year.

- **Organic engagement +323%** (1,300 → 5,500 engagements, May–Oct 2025), **content up +294%** (to ~210 posts/month), across **six platforms** — benchmarked in the **top 15% of nonprofit programs nationally**.
- **TikTok** became the flagship channel (8.5% engagement rate); **LinkedIn** drove 745 clicks to the website.
- Campaign spikes prove the system: DOFL organic impressions grew **22× year-over-year**; Pride organic grew **+121% impressions / +170% interactions**, with **Facebook interactions up 4.4×** YoY.
- Threaded through it all: **HIV wellness / U=U "myth-buster" content** — sexual-health education at scale.

### 3. The rebrand — made real, everywhere staff and clients touch it

**What CARES got:** a modern identity that now lives in the real world, not just a style guide — across sub-brands (Nashville CARES, CARES 360, HealthyU, Prevention/DART).

- Delivered and in-hand: name badges, business cards, email signatures, rack cards, palm cards, table runners, mobile-medical-unit branding, and refreshed sub-brand logos.
- Environmental branding for the **CARES 360 community clinic** is underway (early stage) — the physical space is the next chapter.

### 4. Websites — one live, one on the launch pad

**What CARES got:** a working digital front door for the clinic today, and a rebuilt flagship site about to go live.

- **cares360.org — LIVE.** Home/About/Services/Insurance/Contact, **Athena EHR patient portal**, and **online booking (Phreesia)**, serving Nashville and Memphis. A telehealth landing page is the next addition.
- **nashvillecares.org — launching now.** The rebuilt, rebranded site is fully designed (38/38 pages approved) and built on staging; it's in final QA and client-approval rounds, with the donation integration due mid-July. *(The current live domain still shows the legacy site with a "sneak peek" banner — launch is imminent, not done.)*

### 5. PR — a media engine primed and starting to fire

**What CARES got:** a credible, segmented press strategy and the first earned-media wins.

- Delivered: the **"CARES 2.0" story arc** (a segmented messaging framework), a media-target map (from *The Tennessean* to CNN Health), spokesperson media training, and seasonal pitch calendars.
- Earned: **two DOFL TV segments aired** (NewsChannel5, WSMV) plus a *Nashville Scene* full page. The next step is turning the primed pitch engine into a steady stream of placements.
- Earlier proof point (baseline): **Pride 2025** generated **~$3.49M in publicity value (AVE)** and **~75M UVM across 5 published articles** — early validation that a segmented press push could position CARES as a leader in LGBTQ+ health.

### 6. How the account runs — the machine behind the work

**What CARES got:** a professional operating system — a flexible expert bench, a formal request-intake process, bi-weekly budget burn-down reporting, and a **client-approval workflow that cleared 101/101 items**. A cross-project dashboard is being built (at your team's request) so CARES can see everything in one view.

## What's still ahead — the value we unlock next

Honest and forward-looking. These are next year's biggest opportunities:

- **The donor engine is the single biggest untapped value.** Turning Bloomerang into a donation-generating machine has **not really happened yet** — database cleanup is barely started, no automated donor journeys are live, and the email platform is mid-migration (MailerLite → SharpSpring/Constant Contact). It was deliberately parked behind the website launch. **This is where next year's dollars are:** clean data + automated, segmented donor journeys layered on top of the audiences we've already built.
- **nashvillecares.org goes live** — completing the two-site digital presence.
- **The 40th Anniversary Gala (Nov 14)** — the year's marquee fundraising moment.
- **Fall Prides + AIDS Walk** — redeploying the Pride tent kit and the audiences already captured.
- **Retargeting already in motion** — Pride's geo-fenced audiences are working for the clinic through December; the Gala and World AIDS Day are next.
- **Measurement in one place.** The strongest proof points currently live scattered across Google Sheets, PDFs, and an archived project. Consolidating them into the new dashboard turns "trust us, it worked" into a live, always-on view of impact.

## How this maps to your strategic plan

The engagement is concentrated on **Goal 2 (revitalized brand & marketing)** — and there it delivered strongly: the rebrand is real (2.1), the brand launched publicly (2.2/2.4), social and paid activation are humming (2.2/2.3), events are aligned to the brand (2.4), and the marketing operating system is in place (2.5/2.6).

It also delivered **genuine, if partial, progress on Goals 1 and 3**:
- **1.4 (expand the clinic):** cares360.org live, Memphis market entry, clinic environmental branding underway.
- **1.5 (diversify revenue):** DOFL ($86.6K), the Gala engine, sponsorship packages, and the donor-CRM foundation.
- **3.3 (community education):** HIV/HEP-C brochures, U=U social content, and inclusive Pride messaging.

The parts of Goals 1 and 3 that are clinical or services-based (testing, viral suppression, care partnerships) sit inside Nashville CARES' own operations — outside this marketing engagement — and aren't reflected here by design.
