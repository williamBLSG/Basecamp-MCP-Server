## Part 2 — Landing page (what must be on it)

One page per market (or per service if we scope it that way). Required elements, top to bottom:

1. **Headline that mirrors the ad's promise** — message match, so the visitor knows they're in the right place.
2. **Subhead** — one line reinforcing confidential / free / fast.
3. **Primary CTA, above the fold:**
   - Nashville: "Schedule an appointment" → Phreesia
   - Memphis: "Call to schedule" (click-to-call) → Memphis number
4. **What to expect** — short, plain-language description of the service (e.g., "a 20-minute visit," "a daily pill up to 99% effective at preventing HIV").
5. **Trust signals** — confidential · free/low-cost · serving Middle TN since 1985 · HIPAA-compliant · HEARTLine 1-800-845-4266.
6. **Inclusivity line** — welcoming to every identity and orientation; no judgment.
7. **Secondary CTA** — "Prefer we reach out? Contact us" → Jotform (optional for Memphis).
8. **Repeat the primary CTA** at the bottom.

**Page requirements (for whoever builds it):**
- Strip the main-site navigation and outbound links — the only paths forward are the CTAs.
- Mobile-first and fast; this audience is overwhelmingly on phones.
- Host **off the main site** (privacy).
- Carry **UTM parameters** through to the destination so GA can attribute the click.
- **No third-party pixel** on the schedule/call step without privacy-counsel sign-off.

## Compliance guardrails (non-negotiable)
- Never imply we know the viewer's HIV status, behavior, or orientation. Speak to everyone.

- Follow Meta's healthcare / sensitive-category ad policies. No targeting by health condition (the media buyer targets by **geography only** — Nashville vs Memphis metro).
- Flag anything borderline for legal/clinical review before it ships.
